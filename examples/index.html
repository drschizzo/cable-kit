cable-kit

Thousands of cables for three.js: @drschizzo/avbd-cables simulates them, @drschizzo/three-cable-tubes draws them.

  • Grab: ropes you take and drag with the mouse, held back by a rod and the floor.
  • Falling: hundreds of ropes falling in a heap, the physics in a Web Worker.
  • Tubes only: thousands of tubes animated by hand, no physics.
  • Fibre optics: 16 000 strands moved by a compute shader, never leaving the GPU, with pulses of light (WebGPU).
